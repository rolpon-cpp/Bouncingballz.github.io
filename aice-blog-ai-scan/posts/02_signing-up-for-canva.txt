TITLE: Blog Post #2: Signing up for Canva
DATE: 2026-08-19T19:31:54
MODIFIED: 2026-08-25T19:09:58
URL: https://lalitnunna.edublogs.org/2026/08/19/signing-up-for-canva/

I used my Google Account to sign into Canva, so I could log in without needing a new password. Even better, I already made my Canva account a few years ago! I have used Canva in the past before to create school projects, logos, and videos. When exploring the platform, I noticed it is very much a visual design platform meant for all kinds of media: presentations, spreadsheets, pictures, videos, and so much more. There were all kinds of tools to insert clip art, draw with a pen, and add in color & backgrounds to scenes. There were mountains of templates and ready-to-go slides to work with!

Equipped with these tools, I decided to create a brand new design. I clicked the “Create” button to produce a brand new project. Then, I selected the presentation format since it had the size I wanted. Once inside, I searched for a nice photo. I found an amazing photo of a plant on sunset, and I decided to use it. I added the image to the Canvas and positioned it such that the sun would fall in line with the rule of two-thirds. Finally, I added some text to add additional context to the image & promote the blog.

This design turned out looking really cool, and it’s very interesting to see how easy it is to create good-looking stuff on Canva. I learned how to insert images, write cool text, and choose good-looking resolutions. Canva is a really good tool to make nice looking school presentations and online logos for digital products. I would definitely encourage others to use it, as it allows you to make a wide variety of content quickly and easily. This ease-of-use makes it an effective tool to communicate ideas via visual design, as it allows you to get your ideas out of your mind as-soon-as-possible, and make it look good too!
