TITLE: Blog Post #4: An Analysis of How Magazine Genre Shape Content and Audience
DATE: 2026-08-23T16:07:20
MODIFIED: 2026-08-27T19:01:20
URL: https://lalitnunna.edublogs.org/2026/08/23/an-analysis-of-how-magazine-genre-shape-content-and-audience/

Gaming magazines are mainly focused on video games, specifically commentary on development, news from communities, and studios. The target audience is mainly composed of gamers, game developers, game designers, and people working at indie studios.

As seen above, the cover design of gaming magazines is focused on showing characters, highlights, and cinematic pictures from the latest popular video games. Color schemes usually include white, red, and black alongside the accent colors of the displayed game. The imagery usually includes vast scenes or prominent characters from a popular game. The headlines usually include the magazine’s brand name alongside a title or phrase mentioning a specific video game or a big question about PC specifications and game controversies. The language and tone are usually humorous, relatable and casual while showing mild frustration at problems within games or the industry. Featured topics often include big game releases, game updates, gaming hardware coverage, or fresh game leaks.

These visual conventions are effective at attracting readers, as they build up excitement and are visually appealing. Cinematic imagery from video games piques the interest of readers passing by, and flashy headlines covering big game releases, top games, or leaks build up a certain level of suspense and surprise, suspense that can only be resolved by looking at the magazine and displayed game or games. Red, white, and black colors alongside the subject games’ accent colors help introduce readers to the subject well while maintaining readability in normal paragraph texts. For example, this magazine shows Nintendo’s top 100 games, evoking a level of excitement into the reader. The diverse range of famous Nintendo IPs attracts readers’ attention by featuring their favorite characters.

As seen above, gaming magazines usually use dark, intense, and dangerous imagery. This is intentional, as it gives the magazines and featured games a level of “coolness”. People are attracted to them because they give off the impression of being serious, “epic”, and novel. This attracts and represents people who may have more mundane or boring lives; as intense activities that these people would be unable to do in real life, like fight in a war or navigate dangerous obstacles, are now activities that they can do with the click of a button.

The magazines shown above very much follow genre expectations. They all feature imagery of video games, contain red, black, and white color schemes, cover hardware or games, and oftentimes feature intense characteristics (like a sword or armor). These attract gamers with novel activities and interesting new content that keeps them hooked.
