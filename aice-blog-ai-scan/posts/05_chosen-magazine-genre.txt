TITLE: Blog Post #5: Chosen Magazine Genre
DATE: 2026-08-24T21:08:40
MODIFIED: 2026-08-25T19:09:40
URL: https://lalitnunna.edublogs.org/2026/08/24/chosen-magazine-genre/

I decided to choose to make a magazine about gaming (video games). Gaming magazines cover game creation, new games, and game leaks. Specifically, I want to cover the process of creating games. I chose this genre because I love creating and playing video games, and I am very knowledgeable about video games. There are millions of people out there who enjoy playing games every day, and so a magazine like this would be able to catch many of these people. 

My target audience is going to mainly be teenagers wanting to be involved in game development. They will be around 13-20 years old and be interested in gaming, computers, and design. This audience wants to know how video games are made and how they can take the path for themselves, which compels them to research by reading my magazine and reading articles. My magazine will represent issues in the gaming space in a negative-but-fixable light. I will acknowledge the issue while providing solutions to these problems. I will prefer to protect gamers while protesting against negative forces.

Magazines that inspired me include PC Gamer, Nintendo Force, and Edge. The conventions are to discuss why someone loves or dislikes a genre and big releases in a very casual tone. I plan to utilize these same conventions to make the magazine relatable to gamers. Using informal communication allows readers to connect to the story and builds trust.

I plan to use pictures of computer design prototypes combined with game imagery to catch the attention of gamers. Game imagery will draw in gamers, communicating to them that the magazine is about gaming. The prototypes and code communicate that this magazine goes beyond the surface of what they already know, luring them in to come read the magazine. The design style will mainly rely on making characters look closer to how the designers made them; like showing a photo of Mario next to a 3D designer constructing Mario’s model. To give readers a social media, I can provide them this very blog, or the social media account of well known game studios.

For production choices, I plan to use photography showing what games look like and their development from an outside perspective. I will show designers working on paper to map out game ideas, and sound designers producing sound effects. The color scheme will be on black, white, and red, matching the colors of gaming magazines. The layout will be organized for readability and aesthetics in mind. The typography will rely on normal fonts with stylized fonts for titles to fit in with each topic.
