I created this mood board to help assist me with designing the actual magazine and correctly using images towards my topic. With this mood board, I will be able to find images for my magazine that work well. I will use my selected colors and fonts to keep a consistent design language across the magazine article. I will utilize basic but stylized shapes to make appealing, non-distracting graphics. 

I selected to use 2 images of computer workstations since they represent both my audience’s interests and the general topic very well. They give a physical depiction of the production of game development, allowing readers to better imagine themselves in the shoes of a game developer. For the background, to keep a simple and appealing design language, I went with 3 diagonal stripes of my main color scheme. I decided to use colors like gray and light brown alongside my main 3 colors for variety and cleanliness. I included images of real magazine covers to show what a magazine should look like, as well as a light bulb and hand-drawn ship to represent my audience’s qualities. I included a whiteboard drawing to show how games are designed in the physical world as well. I included a screenshot of a 3D modeler working on creating a character to show a digital depiction of game development work. I put computer code & a character using a computer to show how game development is connected to technology.

This mood board reflects my audience’s interests since it shows how the work and talents used to create games are the very same talents they have. I show common talents like art, music, and coding to help give this effect. I use things that they are already aware of like whiteboard sketching or artistic drawings to help reinforce this idea. I use words like “Artistic” and “Knowledgeable” to help represent them as well.

When designing my magazine, I plan to use my mood board’s focus on physical photos combined with digital ones to help represent the production pipeline. I plan to keep the same simple color and shape palette to keep my magazine useful and appealing at the same time. I will utilize the same fonts to maintain readability and flare. I will use a less packed layout since I will have a lot more open space. 

My mood board helped me build a clear blueprint for my magazine’s design and look. It will help me guide my choices when picking out photos, colors, and special fonts for specific concepts and worlds. It gives me a clear design language that I can comfortably work with to help me design an appealing and readable magazine that captures my audience well.
