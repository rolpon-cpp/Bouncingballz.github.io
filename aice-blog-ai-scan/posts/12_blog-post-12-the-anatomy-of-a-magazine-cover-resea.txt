PC Gamer Battlefield 3 Magazine

The masthead of the magazine is at the center of the image and is mildly prominent as it is put in medium-sized text. The title suggests the magazine will be covering Battlefield 3, the game it claims is “The Saviour Of Shooters”. The main image is one of the protagnist characters from Battlefield 3. They likely choose this character for additional dramatic effect and easy direct address. The facial expression is serious, pose is straight, and heavy tactical gear suggests Battlefield 3 is a dark and gritty war-like shooter game. The camera shot is a medium shot at eye-level angle with direct address, meaning the subject is centered first. This image instantly attracts attention by using its bold stance and clear camera framing to create an emotional idea: this game is real, epic, and serious. This helps support the magazine’s purpose as a in-depth review of Battlefield 3 by making readers excited before they begin reading.

The magazine features many mini-headlines covering many different but related topics. Certain game releases, gaming technology, and How-2-Play guides are included. Words are emphasized via bolding and flashy tags like “KILLER HORROR” or “MASSIVE REVIEW!”. Many emotional and attention grabbing words-and-phrases like “Zombies in Bikinis!”, “How to Save Adventure Games”, “Amazing Tech”, “The Saviour of Shooters” are used to help attract attention to the magazine and its many subtopics. These headlines suggest that the magazine likes to generally focus on game releases, gaming technology, and potentially other gaming-related subtopics. 

Captialized, bolded, modern fonts are used. The typography helps reinforce the game’s dark-and-gritty theme by not adding silly or humorous letters. Red, whire, and black dominate the color scheme, which help make the magazine feel minimalist and modern. This targets a gaming audience who like new, bold, and appealing visual styles. The most noticeable items are the game character and game title. The eye naturally is drawn towards the center, specifically on the character. Less important headers and titles are pushed to the side whilst the main focus of the magazine (the subject, masthand, and brand title) are pushed to the center and top. This helps guide the reader’s attention by first hooking them in with a large and flashy subject, and then leading them around the image to all the mini-headlines.

The intended reader seems to be gamers specifically invested in the shooter game genre. I can infer this from the fact that this magazine says Battlefield 3 is “the saviour of shooters”, implying the reader is bored or unsatisified with existing shooters, which means they likely play video games. They are likely interested in playing shooter games often and being highly competitive with friends or online players. They are most likely male since the dark-and-gritty tone tend to lean more towards being a male quality in our society. The purpose of the magazine seems to inform and entertain, as they reference reviews and flashy graphics. The magazine invokes excitement and seriousness.

This cover is good at attracting attention by using it’s bold title and subject. It clearly communicates the genre by mentioning “Gamer”, shooters, and top-game IP like Battlefield. It appeals to the audience by focusing on minimalism and cool features. Direct address and medium shot as photography choices help frame the subject well. The readability and amount of text could be improved.

PC Gamer Mass Effect Andromeda Magazine

The name of the magazine is PC Gamer with the masthead at the top. It is very prominent as it is placed in a large font. The title instantly suggests the magazine is about gaming. A character from the game “Mass Effect Andromeda” is featured. They likely chose this to get a subject for direct-address and to help give an idea of the game’s style. The image attractions attention via the space-like helmet and Sci-Fi hands and gun. A close-up, direct address shot was used for the character. They likely choose this shot to help give emphasis on the character and game style, clearly communicating to gamers and audiences the genre and excitement behind the game. It supports the magazine’s purpose as well by telling readers that this magazine is a review of Mass Effect Andromeda.

Headlines like “Revealed: The smash RPG’s massive sequel” and “Inside the BioWare RPG of your dreams” help attract attention using impactful and emotional words. These headlines cover other game releases and gaming technology in the form of GPUs. Phrases like “The History of RPGs” help attract attention to the mini headlines. These suggest the magazine likes to generally cover lots of game launches and gaming technology.

Red, blue, black, and white colors are used, which help make the magazine feel modern and stylistic. Bolded, large, normal, and fancy fonts support a modern and bold tone. The eye is naturally drawn in towards the game character helmet, with text and other images placed to the sides to provide space. The game’s title and character are the most noticeable. The many headlines on the sides with the cinematic image in the center help guide the eyes towards the character. The target audience seems to be gamers interested in new gaming releases or people wanting to get into gaming. This is because the magazine covers new game releases, appealing to gamers looking for new games and the magazine also covers gaming tech, appealing to people wishing to get into gaming by buying computer parts. The magazine seems to be made to inform and entertain, with multiple headlines and flashy images used to get readers hooked. The design gives off a serious and exciting mood, as it uses exciting words like “Revealed” and “much more” whilst showing a cinematic game character.

This magazine cover attracts attention well using the cinematic photo of the game character. It helps communicate the magazine genre via big words like “PC Gamer” and sci-fi technology. It appeals to the target audience by using flashy graphics and a modern style. The close-up, direct address shot of the game character was the most effective design choice.

ROGUE NA Fashion Magazine

The name and masthead of the magazine is at the top “ROGUE”, and it is very prominent. The title alone suggests the magazine challenges the status quo and is very stylistic. On the main image, a fashion model is featured as a close-up, direct address shot. This shot was likely chosen to add emphasis to the subject. This helps the audience understand what the magazine is about and how they might be able to look like the subject if they read the magazine. This helps support the genre as it tells people how to look beautiful and various tips on beauty. The facial expression gives off an elegant, stylistic feel. The lighting is a balance between dim and bright, which helps attract readers to the main subject. Mini headlines like “GOT IT COVERED”, “DRESS FOR SUCCESS”, “THE ART OF STYLE” use emotional and bold words to help give the magazine an elegant, artistic feel whilst making readers excited.

Newspaper & print fonts are used to help maintain readability whilst adding an appealing style to the magazine. Warm, light colors like red, brown, and beige help add an elegant and light feel to the magazine. The magazine is lighter on words and uses the subject’s body and hair to naturally move the eyes to the face. The text elements are put to the sides to help add context without disturbing the subject. The target audience seems to be focused mainly on females aged 13-20 who wish to style themselves. This is evident because the magazine chose to put a young woman on the cover, which will naturally attract likewise people. The magazine gives an elegant and exciting tone by using colors and words like elegance, as well as words like “dress for success” and “got it covered”.

Overall, the cover gives feelings of awe and curiosity. It attracts attention via a close-up shot of a fashion model. It clearly communicates the magazine’s genre as fashion via the subject and language around beauty. It appeals to the target audience by giving them beauty advice and an example of what exceptional beauty looks like. The close-up shot on the subject was the most effective photography choice, as it properly provides emphasis to the beauty model.

There are many similarities and differences between the 3 magazines. In all 3 magazines, the cover shot is usually a shot of a character or model taken as a close-up or medium shot. The two gaming magazines follow similar conventions, with both of them featuring cinematic game characters and packed headlines. The fashion magazine sticks to this convention a little less due to the fact the colors are more warm and looser headlines. Usually, the convention of a game character paired with packed headlines tends to appear the most often in the gaming genre. Photography choices differ across genres because each genre picks camera shots and angles most appealing to it’s audience. The fashion magazine used warm colors because they need to make the beauty model look good. The video game magazines are more about creating excitement around tough characters who aren’t focusing on beauty. I believe the second gaming cover was the most effective at capturing readers, as it covers a large range of topics while maintaining stylistic consistency and appeal.

For my own magazine, I plan to use a close-up, medium shot of a disassembled game character. I will use an eye level camera angle. These choices are good because they mimic common gaming magazine conventions (close up cinematic angles) whilst giving me freedom to better communicate my genre (via the disassembled game character).

I will include the magazine conventions of covering a wide range of topics and having a close-up shot of my subject. The fashion cover inspired me the most, as it gives me an idea on how to balance the complexity and compactness of a gaming magazine with the elegance and looseness of a fashion magazine. I will use the large bold fonts, red/white/black color scheme, and large subject in the center for my own magazine. I will use less flashy graphics for my own magazine. 

For the CCR Question, my magazine uses visual conventions like the large bold font and masthead. I will use content conventions like gaming photos and a wide variety of topics, but with my own twist of focusing on the game creation process and using less flashy graphics. These choices will help me attract my audience by copying what works with other magazines while curating my content and graphics to fit teens 14-18 with creative talents.
