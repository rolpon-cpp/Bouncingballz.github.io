## Edge Magazine 2021 Issue 358 (TOC #1)

## Game Player’s Magazine 1989 Issue 1 (TOC 2)

## First Impressions

TOC #1

The first thing the eyes are drawn to is the large “Table of Contents” header, as it is in a large font and is placed at the top-left of the image, which is where are eyes are naturally drawn to when reading text in an image or paragraph. I think the designer wanted to make it clear what the TOC page was supposed to be and to help communicate to readers where to find the stories they want to read. The second and third things I notice are the two tall images on the right of the page for the Deathloop interview and “Apex Legend’s Newest Reveal”. This design guides readers through the page by first putting their eye at the top left, and moving down to all the stories, moving to the right of the page as each story gets a progressively taller image. This visual hierarchy is effective because it guides the reader through the image smoothly whilst informing them of all the relevant stories.

TOC #2

The first thing my eyes are drawn to are the images at the top, which contain game screenshots and retro art. I think the designer wanted to catch reader’s attention with real video games, making the magazine feel more fun and connected to their interests. The second and third things I notice are the large purple “CONTENTS” text spanning vertically on the left side of the page and the two large transparent green and yellow squares in the background. The design guides readers through the page, first starting at the top with all the images and “CONTENT” caption, then moving down to the list of stories in the magazine. This visual hierarchy is less effective since it feels a lot more cluttered and less visually organized, with inconsistent patterns with the arrangement of elements.

## Evaluating Design Decisions

Layout and Organization (TOC #1)

I think the designer intentionally organized the page in a left-to-right manner to help easily guide readers through the table of contents and to remain modern-looking. This layout helps readers find information quickly since it’s all concentrated to path of the eyes looking at the image. The slowly increasing height of the images and minimalistic style (less colors and fonts) are by far the most effective, as they create a natural path for the eyes to follow and stops the magazine from feeling old or unofficial. Although, some could argue that is it a little too minimalistic, so adding more visual elements organized in the right ways could significantly improve the perception of the TOC. The overall layout is successful at correctly communicating to readers all the relevant stories and how to find them without looking poor or odd to look at.

Layout and Organization (TOC #2)

I think the designer intentionally organized the page in a simple, book-style manner is to help make the magazine feel familiar and easy-to-read. The list-like layout is much simpler and recognizable than other magazines, sticking to graphic-book like conventions. This layout helps readers find info quickly by reusing a format they are already accustomed to and not making them use more mental effort to understand a visually complex TOC. The simple list and images at the top are the best aspects, as they are easy-to-understand and hook readers in. Aspects like the color choice, visual element count, and caption placement could be changed to look more modern and better flow with the eyes, like cutting out small subline texts, removing background grid and objects, transforming the Contents caption to be at the top-left of the image, and more. The layout is overall successful at communicating to readers where all the magazine articles are but could be improved with better color and visual element choice.

Images (TOC #1)

I believe the images in TOC #1 were chosen because each image was the corresponding image to one of the magazine stories. The images support the magazine’s genre by hinting to readers/onlookers that the magazine has video games inside it, which logically leads to the magazine’s genre being video games. The images also appeal to the target audience by showing the audience games and characters they like. The image on the right is the most effective since it is the tallest image in the TOC, and thus has the most emphasis and power. The images don’t communicate any specific message; instead, they help add context, appeal, and recognizability to each of the magazine stories. 

Images (TOC #2)

I believe the images in TOC #2 were chosen to help add appeal and excite readers with the games they might see inside the magazine. The images support the magazine’s genre and appeals to the target audience by showing genre-appropriate imagery and giving readers an idea of what some of the games look like. There is no particularly effective or focus image, instead there is a grid of 4 images with varying sizes and positions. The images help communicate what the featured games will look like to help readers get an idea of what they’re about to see.

Typography (TOC #1)

The fonts on TOC #1 were chosen to help maintain minimalism and consistency with the basic color scheme and lack of visual elements. These fonts help reinforce the magazine’s identity as a informational and straight-to-the-point magazine by not causing visual clutter. This contributes to the overall tone as a neutral and minimal magazine. The typography choice to switch to a more stylized font for the story sublines help add a bit of style and flare to a dull image to make it still look nice, even with less visual elements. I would also make the table of contents have more unique typography as well to add visual flare. 

Typography (TOC #2)

The fonts on TOC #2 were chosen to help keep simplicity. The whole TOC only uses 1 font, just with different colors. This helps reinforce the magazine’s identity and tone as simple and book-like. No particular typography choices are used to help enhance the TOC or add visual flare. I would definitely change some of the fonts to be more unique and stylistic to help add appeal for readers.

Colors and Design (TOC #1)

The colors (being black, white, and yellow) help support the magazine’s genre and objective as a minimalist and informative magazine with no visual clutter or fluff. These colors naturally appeal to the audience since they establish a design consistency framework for the rest of the magazine, keeping an appealing design throughout the magazine. No specific emotions are ideas are communicated via the colors, they were chosen to be simple and easily keep consistency throughout the magazine. The color scheme is very successful as it establishes an appealing design framework throughout the magazine that is easy for the designer to stick to.

Colors and Design (TOC #2)

The colors (being white, purple, yellow, black, and green) support the magazine’s genre and objective with a variety of colors for varied visual elements, reflecting the wide variety of games the magazine covers. These colors appeal to the audience by adding lots of visual content and real imagery to see. The colors don’t invoke any special or emotional feelings, the colors were selected to help add lots of visual elements. The color scheme works but could be improved with more complementary colors and better color arrangement.

## Audience and Effectiveness

TOC #1

The target audience is people who are interested in the latest game releases and what people think of them. This is evident because TOC #1 has a lot of game reviews and has a few special stories like interviews, which shows they are trying to appeal to people who want opinions on a variety of games. The TOC appeals to this audience by clearly showing them where all the reviews are located and putting a large chunk of the review in succession, so readers don’t need to skip around the magazine too much to read each review. The TOC is successful in meeting the needs of its audience by clearly arranging and showing the reviews to be convenient for readers.

TOC #2

The target audience is kids, teenagers, or young adults who want to learn about all the different spheres and communities of gaming. This is evident by the fact that the magazine covers a lot of gaming platform groups, with few stories outside of talking about gaming platform groups. The TOC appeals to this audience by giving them lots of examples and explanations on what these other gamers on other platforms do to play games, covering the advantages and disadvantages of each platform. The TOC is overall successful as it covers a wide variety of gaming-related topics, informing its target audience about the gaming world. 

## Comparing the two TOCs

I think TOC #1 has the strongest layout overall, as it looks a lot more stylistically consistent and modern than TOC #2. TOC #1 wins in color scheme as it uses high-contrast, clean colors vs. TOC #2 which has a lot more confusing, less clean colors. TOC #2 is easier to navigate thanks to it using a familiar book-like layout, whilst TOC #1 uses a more confusing left-to-right layout, but this is a minor win for TOC #2. TOC #1 has a much better use of images as well, as the images look like they actually match the discussed stories, vs TOC #2 which has 4, almost random feeling images at the top that don’t seem to support a specific purpose. TOC #1 has a more appealing visual hierarchy as well, as it uses a unique and clear left-to-right arrangement while TOC #2 has a more book-styled up-to-down arrangement. TOC #1 is just more appealing to the audience because it looks cleaner and is arranged in a unique way (i. e. cleaner colors, unique left-to-right arrangement).

## Design Decisions for my own TOC

Design Choices I Will Use

I plan to include the simplicity of TOC #1’s colors, fonts, and left-to-right placement in my own TOC. I believe these features are effective because they keep appeal whilst making design easier for me. TOC #1 mainly inspired all of these changes.

Design Choices I Will Avoid

TOC #2’s design choices to use a familiar book style and more color variety makes the magazine feel less unique and less clean. They do not work well because they are boring and conflicting to readers. I will avoid these mistakes by sticking to a simple 3 color schema and always maintaining a minimalist style.

Research-Informed Decisions

I plan to include a 3-color scheme because it effectively helped readers to learn the magazine’s design style quickly and add a level of recognizability to my magazine. I will avoid using lots of colors because it made TOC #2 less effective by looking more confusing, random, and unclean. My audience research suggests that I should stick to a few fonts and cut down on visual flare because my research indicated people like more straight-to-the-point content.

## Reflection

The most effective TOC feature I encountered was the left-to-right contents arrangement of TOC #1. The least effective one was the varied color scheme of TOC #2. This activity helped me understand magazine design a lot more by analyzing what makes a magazine look good or bad. This analysis taught me valuable skills and conventions to use when designing my own TOC. Evaluating media products before before making your own media product is important since you can learn a lot from what has worked in the past vs. what has failed in the past.
