Layout Analysis

I think TOC Flat Plan #2 has the most effective layout. The layout was simple and non-cluttered, yet it had clearly labeled sections to list out articles and add in a main image. This makes the layout very simple for readers to quick look at and instantly get to the information they want. The design choices of splitting the main image and actual contents into two different pages makes the page easy to read while maintaining visual flare by not confusing readers with a cluttered background.

Visual Design

Photos are used in all TOC Flat plans to maintain reader interest via visual simulation and provide readers with a visual idea of what to expect up ahead. I think TOC Flat Plan #1’s photo placement is the best, since it gives lots of space for images to help represent each category. Images support the magazine’s content by adding a real picture or idea that the reader can imagine in their head vividly without needing to first read through a lengthy visual description.

Organization

In all 3 Flat Plans, Page Numbers, Headlines, and Sublines were organized very differently. In Flat Plan #1, the headlines, page numbers, and sublines were all contained into the box next to the images. In Flat Plan #2, the headline & subline is located at the top of the second page, with the page numbers being inside the “Article Column” sections. In Flat Plan #3, the headlines, sublines, and page numbers are all inside numerous “Different Pages” boxes, which are spread vertically across the image. The categories in the TOCs help organize different types of stories and articles together, making it easier for readers to read about only the topics they care about. In my own TOC, I will organize different sections of the game development process and types of processes into a few main categories.

Planning For My TOC

I will use the multi-page design element from TOC #2 in my own TOC. I will avoid compacting lots of information into lots of tiny boxes like TOC #3. My TOC will reflect my magazine audience and genre by showing relevant topics and stories to game development, targeted towards individuals aged 14-18 who are creative and interested in game development. I want readers to notice my main image first when they look at my TOC. 

Reflection

I learned good styling conventions and layouts for TOCs in my research of TOC flat plans. TOC Layout #2 inspired me the most since it was the most visually appealing with its organized structure and wide space for the main image. This research influenced my design decisions by giving me ideas on how to make good looking magazine covers. This activity will help me make a professional looking TOC by showing me example of what has worked in the past, allowing me to smoothly adapt those elements into my own magazine while remaining unique and original via my custom stories and audience.
